Flutter framework: https://github.com/flutter/flutter (BSD 3-Clause). Material Icons: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons (Apache 2.0). Cupertino Icons: https://pub.dev/packages/cupertino_icons . The simple sparkle launcher graphic is project-local artwork.
